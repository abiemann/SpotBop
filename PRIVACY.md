# SpotBop Privacy Summary

SpotBop does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or otherwise disclose personal
information or other user data to the developer or third parties.

The Android game stores scores, optional three-letter initials, settings, and
first-launch state locally in Android private app storage. It requests vibration
access for haptic feedback and offers optional Do Not Disturb access during
gameplay. It does not request Internet access and contains no advertising,
analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.

Read the complete published policy at:

<https://abiemann.github.io/spotbop-privacy/>
